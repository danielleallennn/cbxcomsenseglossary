# Comsense Glossary

A quick-reference glossary for common terms, fields, and concepts used in Comsense.

## About This Glossary

The Comsense Glossary was created to make unfamiliar terminology easier to understand and provide a consistent reference point for learners and team members working in Comsense.

Use this glossary when you:

- Encounter an unfamiliar Comsense term
- Need a quick reminder of what a field or concept means
- Want to confirm terminology while completing a task
- Need additional support after training

## How to Use It

Search or browse the glossary to find the term you need.

Each entry is written to provide a clear, practical explanation of the term and how it relates to working in Comsense.

> **Tip:** Use your browser's **Find** feature (`Ctrl + F` on Windows or `Command + F` on Mac) to quickly locate a specific term.

## Glossary

Glossary terms and definitions are maintained within this repository.

As Comsense processes, terminology, or training materials evolve, entries may be added or updated to keep this resource useful and relevant.

## A Learning Resource

This glossary is designed as a performance-support resource, not a replacement for established Comsense procedures or business processes.

When you need more detailed guidance, refer to the appropriate training materials, process documentation, or other approved resources.
